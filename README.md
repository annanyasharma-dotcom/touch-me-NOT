# Touch Me Not

A touchless interface made in honour of the touch-me-not plant. Every UI element, including the title, moves away when your hand comes close.

It uses the front camera and MediaPipe Hand Landmarker to track your fingertips, then maps them to screen coordinates and pushes nearby elements away with a small spring physics loop. Touch and mouse work as a fallback.

Open the deployed link on your phone, tap **Use camera**, and hover your hand over the screen. The camera needs an https:// page.
